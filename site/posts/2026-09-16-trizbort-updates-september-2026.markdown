Trizbort has had some quality-of-life updates by [Dan Fabulich](https://github.com/dfabulich). Thanks Dan!

* When adding objects to one room, and then double-clicking on another room, the Room panel was opened to the Objects tab, rather than its first tab. This is annoying behavior; now the panel always opens to the Room panel.
* The Escape key now closes non-focusable panels (Objects tab)
* When creating a new map, Trizbort always shows a Welcome tip, a "First room" tip, a "Connecting rooms" tip, and a "Container objects" tip. That's a lot of tips! Now, the user can just click "Don't show tips" on the first tip.
* Keyboard shortcuts were added to create rooms in the up/down directions.
